Teacher: record the letter sounds

Computer voices can say letter names and words, but not single letter sounds. Record each sound once so the Sonido buttons and blending play your voice. Say just the sound, short and clean, with no "uh" after it (/k/ not "kuh").

Recordings made here play on this device only. To make them play for every child, tap Download on each one and upload the files to the sounds folder of the GitHub repo.

Children's chorus (optional): record up to three groups of children singing the ABC song. When you tap Record, the song plays so the children stay in time, and recording stops when the song ends. Their voices then play along every time the song is sung. Get written permission from parents before recording children.
