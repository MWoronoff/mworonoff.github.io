Market Opportunity Drill-Down methodology
Opportunity Score = 35% Apartment Market Scale + 30% Apartment Concentration + 20% Occupancy Strength + 15% Property Marketing Opportunity. ZIP components are normalized within the selected metro; counties are aggregated first and then scored against other counties in that metro. All matching ZIPs remain available.

Property ZIP Radius Analysis

Enter a property ZIP and radius. Every ZIP whose centroid falls within the radius is included and ranked; no minimum-score exclusion is applied.

Scoring weights: Recommended (45 / 30 / 25) · Adjust
Property Marketing Opportunity: 45% Target Apartment Audience + 30% Renter Scale + 25% Marketing Need Custom weighting active
Total: 100%. Custom weights must total 100%.
Using Custom Weights: The recommended 45% Target Apartment Audience / 30% Renter Scale / 25% Marketing Need weighting is the standard benchmark. Adjusting the weights fine-tunes prioritization among ZIPs based on your campaign strategy; it does not change the underlying demographic or apartment-market data.
How to read this score: Target Apartment Audience reflects the ZIP's apartment-oriented advertising signal; Renter Scale measures renter-household size relative to the other ZIPs in the selected radius; Marketing Need identifies ZIPs where additional advertising may have greater value. Distance does not affect opportunity ranking. It is applied separately to Recommended Budget Share.

Property ZIP Radius methodology
Every ZIP in the radius remains in the ranking. Recommended Property Marketing Opportunity = 45% Target Apartment Audience + 30% Renter Scale + 25% Marketing Need; users may customize those three weights if they total 100%. Distance is excluded from opportunity and applied separately to Recommended Budget Share. Suggested dollars appear only when a budget is supplied.
Current data source: Apartment Management Market Opportunity workbook. Nationwide results currently include 939 metros/CBSAs and 3,199 counties. This build uses one national Market Health ranking for metro comparison and expansion screening, a metro drill-down that ranks counties or ZIPs inside the selected market, market comparison/export, and the property ZIP-radius advertising module with a configurable Property Marketing Opportunity score and separately distance-decayed budget allocation. True historical rent/vacancy series and active multifamily pipeline data remain the next major underwriting enhancement.
