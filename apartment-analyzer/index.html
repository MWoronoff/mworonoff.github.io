How to use the rankings: Expansion / Acquisition Potential screens for markets with stronger renter demand and portfolio fundamentals. Disposition / Review Priority surfaces markets that merit closer portfolio review because of supply pressure, vacancy and weaker fundamentals. Leasing / Marketing Need identifies markets where competitive or leasing pressure may justify greater marketing support. These are directional screening tools, not property valuations or transaction recommendations.

Property marketing methodology: every ZIP in the selected radius remains in the ranking. The Property Marketing Score blends Property Advertising Score, renter-household scale within the selected radius, Marketing Need, and proximity to the property. The ranking uses one evidence-based weighting because the former campaign-objective variants produced essentially the same ZIP rankings in nationwide testing. Suggested budget then applies an additional distance-decay factor so closer ZIPs receive more weight when otherwise similar. This is a media-planning aid, not a required spend prescription.
Current data source: Apartment Management Market Opportunity workbook. Nationwide results currently include 939 metros/CBSAs and 3,199 counties. This build includes national metro/county rankings, market comparison, export, and the property ZIP-radius advertising module with a single Property Marketing Priority score and distance-decayed budget allocation. National rankings have been simplified to three evidence-supported objectives; ZIP analysis uses one Property Marketing Priority ranking after nationwide overlap testing showed the former campaign variants were effectively redundant. True historical rent/vacancy series and active multifamily pipeline data remain the next major underwriting enhancement.
