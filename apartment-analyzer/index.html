How to use the rankings: Scores combine renter-market depth, apartment-market fundamentals, vacancy, recent construction and other current conditions. They are designed to help prioritize markets for discussion and further research—not to replace property-level financial analysis or investment underwriting.

How the ZIP ranking works: every ZIP in the selected radius remains in the list. The ZIP Marketing Score combines advertising opportunity, renter-household scale, marketing opportunity and proximity to the property. The campaign objective changes the weighting. Suggested budget shares give somewhat more weight to nearby ZIPs when other factors are similar. Use this as a media-planning guide rather than a required spending formula.
About the data: Nationwide results currently include 939 metro areas and 3,199 counties. Rankings use current renter, housing, vacancy, rent, recent-construction and related market indicators to support directional market screening. Property ZIP-radius planning includes all ZIPs in the chosen radius and can suggest relative advertising allocations. Historical rent/vacancy trends, active construction pipelines and property-level financials should be reviewed separately for investment decisions.
