What the Model Measures

Broadcast: Scarborough's actual measured A18+ audience plus the supplied Gender, Age and HHI profile cuts for ABC, CBS, CW, FOX and NBC across available half-hour periods. Broadcast defaults to a $7.00 A18+ Broadcast Planning CPM, which is editable. Total Broadcast impressions are calculated from spend and that CPM; the selected demographic's measured composition determines target impressions, waste, effective target CPM, GRPs and CPP.

Frequency & campaign length: Broadcast defaults to 3.0× per week and Digital Video defaults to 2.0× per week; both are editable. Campaign frequency = weekly frequency × campaign length (default 4 weeks), so 3.0× weekly Broadcast frequency equals 12.0× over a four-week campaign. Broadcast target reach = target impressions ÷ Broadcast campaign frequency; Digital target reach = Digital impressions ÷ Digital campaign frequency. Both are capped at the selected demographic universe.

Digital Video: CPM defaults to $45 and is editable. Digital impressions = spend ÷ CPM × 1,000.

Combined campaign: Spend, target impressions, wasted impressions, target efficiency and effective target CPM are aggregated across the selected Broadcast and Digital Video allocations. Combined reach and frequency are intentionally not modeled because the available Scarborough streaming measures describe Broadcast delivery paths rather than duplication between Broadcast ad exposure and a separately purchased Digital Video campaign. No cross-platform duplication rate is assumed.

Targeting: Gender and age use Scarborough’s measured Broadcast audience and universe. HHI uses the uploaded DMA-level Scarborough distribution for Adults 18+. When HHI is selected, the model applies that DMA HHI share to the selected gender/age audience and universe. This is an estimated cross-target based on an independence assumption, not a measured Scarborough age × gender × HHI cross-tab.

Waste: Wasted impressions = total Broadcast impressions − target Broadcast impressions. Target efficiency = target impressions ÷ total impressions. Broadcast waste is based on the resulting target composition. Digital delivery is treated as targeted to the selected gender, age and HHI target.

Build 3 Guardrails

HHI guardrail: HHI percentages are measured at the DMA A18+ level; combined age/gender/HHI delivery is modeled, not directly cross-tabbed. Use an actual Scarborough cross-tab if available for client-facing precision.

Combined TV + Digital deduplicated reach is intentionally not fabricated. The analyzer reports Digital reach independently and Broadcast GRPs/impressions. A cross-platform duplication model will be added only when we adopt a defensible assumption or source.

Pricing: Broadcast defaults to a $7.00 A18+ planning CPM and Digital Video defaults to $45 CPM; both are editable. Replace either benchmark with client-specific CPMs when available. The application immediately recalculates impressions, target efficiency, effective target CPM, GRPs and delivery.
