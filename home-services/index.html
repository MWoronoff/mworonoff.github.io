How is Opportunity calculated?

Media Market

30% Replacement-Ready Households + 25% Category Demand Signal + 25% Structural Category Need + 20% Market Scale.

Local Opportunity

40% Replacement-Ready + 35% category structural need + 25% addressable household scale.

Interpretation

Scores are relative 0–100 opportunity measures, not forecasts of leads, sales, or revenue.
