Current NOAA/CPC 90-Day Outlook

These are NOAA Climate Prediction Center's latest official three-month outlook maps. They show probabilities of above-normal, near-normal, or below-normal temperature and precipitation—not forecasts of specific weather events or home-service demand.

Use them as an additional planning signal when evaluating HVAC, Roofing, and Windows & Doors opportunities.

Temperature Outlook

Warmer- or cooler-than-normal seasonal conditions may help inform the timing and emphasis of HVAC and energy-efficiency campaigns.

Precipitation Outlook

Wetter- or drier-than-normal seasonal conditions may provide useful context for roofing and exterior-home-service planning.

Source: NOAA / National Weather Service, Climate Prediction Center. Official 90-day outlooks are issued monthly near mid-month. NOAA Climate Prediction Center
