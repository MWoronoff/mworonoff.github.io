NOAA's current three-month outlook

These are the latest official 90-day outlook maps from NOAA's Climate Prediction Center. Shaded areas show the chance that the next three months will be above, near or below normal for temperature and precipitation. They are probabilities, not forecasts of specific weather events or of home-service demand.

Temperature outlook

Warmer- or cooler-than-normal outlooks can help decide when to lean into HVAC tune-up, replacement and energy-efficiency campaigns.

Precipitation outlook

Wetter- or drier-than-normal outlooks give context for roofing, gutter, waterproofing and other exterior-service planning.

How to use this with the analyzer

  • Rank markets in the Home Services Market Analyzer first, then check whether those markets fall in an area NOAA expects to be unusually hot, cold, wet or dry.
  • Use the outlook to time flights and messaging within the top markets, not to pick the markets themselves.
  • NOAA updates these maps monthly, around the third Thursday, so check back before planning each new quarter.

Source: NOAA / National Weather Service, Climate Prediction Center, official 90-day outlooks. The maps on this page always show NOAA's latest release.
