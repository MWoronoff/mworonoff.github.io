# Archived October 2026

The original Apartment Management Market Analyzer, kept for reference. It still runs at /apartment-analyzer/archive-2026-10/.

It was replaced by the Apartment Market Analyzer (/apartment-analyzer/), built on the shared analyzer engine, because this
version had no DMA for any ZIP, scored ZIPs with no residents, used rents and incomes about a decade old, and had
undocumented data sources.
