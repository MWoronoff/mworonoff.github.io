What the Model Measures

Broadcast: Scarborough's measured audience and universe define the selected target. Target Demo CPP is estimated from a generic A18+ planning CPP and the selected Gender/Age universe; the advertiser can override that estimate. Broadcast GRPs = Broadcast spend ÷ Target Demo CPP; target impressions = GRPs × target universe ÷ 100; effective target CPM is then derived from Broadcast spend and target impressions.

Frequency & Reach: Broadcast defaults to 3.0×/week and Digital Video to 2.0×/week. Campaign frequency = weekly frequency × campaign length. Reach incorporates standard diminishing returns scaling and is capped at the selected demographic universe.

Digital Video: CPM defaults to $45 and is editable. Digital impressions = spend ÷ CPM × 1,000.

Combined Campaign: Spend, target impressions, wasted impressions, target efficiency, and effective target CPM are aggregated across channels. Combined reach/frequency are intentionally not modeled due to lack of standard cross-platform duplication assumptions.

HHI & Targeting: Gender/age use measured Scarborough audience. HHI applies DMA-level distribution as a modeled cross-target estimate based on an independence assumption.

Planning Guardrails

HHI Guardrail: HHI percentages are measured at the DMA A18+ level; combined age/gender/HHI delivery is modeled, not directly cross-tabbed. Use a direct Scarborough cross-tab when available for client-facing precision.

Deduplicated Reach: Combined TV + Digital deduplicated reach is intentionally not fabricated. The analyzer reports Digital reach and Broadcast reach independently.

Pricing Logic: Broadcast uses an automatically estimated, editable Target Demo CPP rather than a raw CPM input. Digital Video defaults to $45 CPM and remains editable.
