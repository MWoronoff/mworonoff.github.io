13 East 47th Street · New York City
The Story

A supper club in the heart of postwar Manhattan

In the late 1940s and early 1950s, The Panda operated at 13 East 47th Street in Midtown Manhattan, on the north side of 47th Street between Fifth and Madison Avenues.

Michael “Mike” Ronzitti entered the business with Andy and Gus. Family history holds that the three men acquired the existing establishment together. Mike later bought out his partners and became the sole proprietor.

The surviving material describes The Panda as more than a simple restaurant. Its own advertising promoted cocktails, dinner, supper, music, entertainment and dancing — the language of a small Midtown supper club.

The Photograph

The Panda rediscovered

After decades without a known exterior photograph of the restaurant, an image was located in the Lloyd Acker collection at The New York Public Library.

The photograph documents the building and storefront at 13 East 47th Street. NYPL staff described the undated image as appearing to date from the late 1940s.

It is the most important surviving visual record yet located of The Panda during the period associated with Mike Ronzitti.

The Panda Restaurant, 13 East 47th Street, Manhattan. Lloyd Acker collection, New York Public Library.
Surviving Evidence

The Panda in its own words

One surviving restaurant card provides a remarkable snapshot of the business.

It identifies The Panda Restaurant, Inc. at 13 East 47th Street, New York City and names Mike, Andy and Gus.

Cocktails · Dinner · Supper
Music · Entertainment · Dancing

The card also records the restaurant's telephone number: ELdorado 5-7808.

Restaurant Branding

The panda itself

The restaurant's surviving matchbooks confirm that the panda was not simply a name — it was part of The Panda's visual identity.

The matchbooks display a panda illustration, the restaurant name, the 13 East 47th Street address and the ELdorado 5-7808 telephone number.

They also promote one of the restaurant's specialties: Shrimp à la Panda.

Timeline

What we know so far

1941

The earlier storefront

A New York City tax photograph records this stretch of East 47th Street before the period associated with Mike Ronzitti. The architecture and storefront configuration provide an important visual reference for identifying 13 East 47th Street.

Late 1940s

Mike, Andy and Gus

Mike Ronzitti, Andy and Gus are associated together with The Panda. A surviving restaurant card names all three men.

Circa 1949–1951

Mike becomes proprietor

According to family history, Mike later bought out Andy and Gus and became The Panda's sole proprietor.

1951

Mike Ronzitti dies

Mike Ronzitti died at The Panda in 1951, bringing a short but important chapter in the family's New York history to an end.

Today

The archive grows

Photographs, newspapers, government records and family material are being assembled to reconstruct the restaurant's story.

Ongoing Research

Help us find more

We are continuing to search for photographs, menus, advertisements, matchbooks, postcards, newspaper stories and personal memories of The Panda Restaurant at 13 East 47th Street, particularly from approximately 1949–1951.

We are especially interested in additional exterior or interior photographs of The Panda, photographs of East 47th Street between Fifth and Madison Avenues, menus, advertisements, employee photographs and memories from people who visited or worked at the restaurant.

The archive will be updated as additional evidence is located and verified.

Some dates and ownership details on this site are based on family testimony and continuing historical research. Archival material is identified separately where possible.
