The story of a small Manhattan restaurant, the family behind it, and an effort to recover a nearly forgotten piece of New York history.

13 East 47th Street · New York City
The Story

A restaurant in the heart of postwar Manhattan

In the years following World War II, The Panda operated at 13 East 47th Street in Midtown Manhattan, on the north side of 47th Street between Fifth and Madison Avenues.

Michael “Mike” Ronzitti, together with Gus and Andy, acquired the existing restaurant and retained its name — The Panda — in hopes of keeping the establishment's existing customers. Mike later bought out his partners and became sole proprietor.

Today, the surviving photographs, documents, newspaper references, matchbooks and family memories are being brought together to reconstruct the restaurant's history and the lives connected to it.

The Photograph

The Panda rediscovered

After decades without a known photograph of the restaurant, an image was located in the Lloyd Acker collection at The New York Public Library.

The photograph shows The Panda at 13 East 47th Street. NYPL staff estimated that the undated image appears to date from the late 1940s.

This image provides the most important surviving visual record yet located of the restaurant during the period associated with the Ronzitti family.

The Panda Restaurant, 13 East 47th Street, New York. Lloyd Acker collection, New York Public Library.
Timeline

What we know so far

1941

Before the Ronzitti ownership

A New York City tax photograph records the storefront at 13 East 47th Street before Mike Ronzitti, Gus and Andy acquired the restaurant. The earlier establishment already operated as The Panda.

Late 1940s

The Panda enters the Ronzitti story

Mike Ronzitti, Gus and Andy acquire The Panda and retain the established restaurant name.

Circa 1949–1951

Mike becomes proprietor

Mike later purchases the interests of Gus and Andy and becomes the restaurant's sole proprietor.

1951

Mike Ronzitti dies

Mike Ronzitti dies at The Panda, bringing a short but important chapter of the family's New York story to an end.

Today

The search continues

Family records, archives, newspapers and photographic collections continue to be searched for additional images and documents connected with The Panda.

Ongoing Research

Help us find more

We are continuing to search for photographs, menus, advertisements, matchbooks, postcards, newspaper stories and personal memories of The Panda Restaurant at 13 East 47th Street, particularly from approximately 1949–1951.

If you have material relating to the restaurant, the surrounding businesses on East 47th Street, or the Ronzitti family, please consider sharing it so it can become part of this historical record.
