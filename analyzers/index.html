Sales guide: how to use these in a pitch

Each analyzer scores every DMA, metro and ZIP for a category's market potential, using Census, CDC, Department of Education, NOAA, Zillow and EASI consumer spending data. Its ZIP Code Radius Tool then counts the exact audience around any location.

  1. Find the strongest marketsRank DMAs and metros by Market Potential Score for the client's category.
  2. Drill into ZIPsSee which ZIPs inside a market score highest, and why.
  3. Size the audience around a locationPick a ZIP and radius, choose the audience, and export the ranked ZIP list.
