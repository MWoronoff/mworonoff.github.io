What it means for local advertisers:

Category analyzers

Each analyzer ranks DMAs, metros, counties and ZIPs by Market Potential Score for one category, shows why an area scores well, and sizes the audience in any trade area, and builds the plan and proposal.

Planning tools for any category

These work alongside every analyzer: build a ZIP target list around a location, measure a client's own customers against the market, and plan a move from broadcast TV into digital video. Each analyzer's trade area builds the recommended media allocation.

Where the numbers come from

Every score starts from published government and licensed data. Trade-area audiences and reach and frequency are modeled from it, and labeled as estimates. Revisions are made monthly; each analyzer's "How scores work" lists its data years.
