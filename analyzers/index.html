What it means for local advertisers:

Category analyzers

Each analyzer ranks DMAs, metros, counties and ZIPs by Market Potential Score for one category and shows why an area scores well. It also sizes the audience in any trade area and builds the plan and proposal.

Planning tools for any category

These work alongside every analyzer: build a ZIP target list around a location, measure a client's own customers against the market, and plan a move from broadcast TV into digital video. Each analyzer's trade area builds the recommended media allocation.

Trade Area Tool

Pick a ZIP and a radius, choose the audience, and get the people in range, how they respond to ads, and a ranked ZIP list to export.

Upload a client's customer list or a Google Ads or Meta report. See where they over- and under-perform, what their best ZIPs share, and look-alike ZIPs to target. The file never leaves the browser.

Where the numbers come from

Every score starts from published government and licensed data. Trade-area audiences and reach and frequency are modeled from it, and labeled as estimates. Market data is rebuilt as each source releases new figures, and the economic snapshot and TV viewing update monthly; each analyzer's "How scores work" lists its data years.
